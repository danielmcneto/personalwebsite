2024 NATIONALS ROBOT

The Build: Autonomous EV3 rescue robot engineered for the 2024 OBR competition, built to solve dynamic line navigation and victim sorting under strict hardware constraints.
The Hardware: Hacked a standard EV3 port to drive two legacy RCX motors simultaneously, doubling torque and speed for the claw mechanism.
The Victim Classifier: Designed a custom crab-claw mechanism lined with copper wire that instantly identified conductive "live" victims by using the metal bodies to complete an analog circuit on contact.

LEGO Mindstorms EV3, Custom Hardware Modification, Analog Sensing, State Machine Logic, Mechanical Design. Custom 3D Pieces.
